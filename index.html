EpoxyMath

Honest epoxy math: the void decides the volume, the product decides the depth.

The volume

Measure the void, not the table. River width x table length x slab depth, plus a waste margin for the drips up the mixing cup, and you get exact resin and hardener in fluid ounces - split at the product's mix ratio.

The depth limit

Every epoxy has a maximum layer depth, and it is chemistry, not marketing: too deep in one pour and the exotherm cracks the piece or worse. EpoxyMath checks your depth against the product and tells you when to pour in layers.

The topcoat

A 1/16-inch flood coat over the whole top, sized separately, in topcoat epoxy - because deep-pour resin in a thin film never cures hard. Cost per pour included, so the kit size is a decision, not a surprise.
