The pour

Measure the VOID, not the table: for a river table that is river width x table length x slab depth. Odd shapes: pour rice or water in, measure that, and enter the dimensions that match the volume.

Your mix

Flood coat the top

A 1/16-inch flood coat over the whole top, in topcoat epoxy - never deep-pour resin for this, it stays rubbery in thin films.

The honest part

Mix ratios are by VOLUME unless the jug says weight - and if you mix a 2:1 resin at 2:1 by weight, you get a permanent sticky mess, because resin and hardener have different densities. Pour deeper than the product's rated layer and the exotherm does the rest: smoking, cracking, sometimes fire. Depth limits are chemistry, not marketing.
